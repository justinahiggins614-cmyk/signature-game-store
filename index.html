WELCOME TO THE STORE

This is The Signature Game Store — a real, working game shop built by Manon. Every game on the shelves is playable right here in your browser, or downloadable as real code you can keep. The collection runs from 1970s arcade-style cabinets through 90s puzzle eras to modern combat-style ops — plus Signature remakes of classic game styles (in the style of the greats, never copying them), a starship multiverse of space-opera adventures, and brand-new originals found nowhere else.

Our mission: a million games, every one with its own cover, its own description, and its own AI companion from the Signature AI Phone Book. The store is open around the clock — pick a game and play.
