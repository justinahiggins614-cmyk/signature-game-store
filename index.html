    Welcome to The Signature Game Store

    Real playable games — pick an era, press PLAY, and play right in your browser. Here’s how to use it:

    1. Browse by era. Six graphics eras — 6-BIT, 32-BIT, PLAYSTATION, 64, PS3, BEYOND — each game’s look steps up through gaming history.
    2. Open a game. Cover, what it is, how to win — plus the Game Master AI pal for tips and the real game code.
    3. Press PLAY. The game boots on the page — touch controls, score, game over, restart. The era dial changes the look live.
    4. Game Archive. The full A–Z catalog lives on the archive tab — the front page stays lean.
